FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about custom video editing requests.

What types of videos can I request?

You can request YouTube Shorts, Reels, cricket edits, gaming montages, long-form YouTube videos, cinematic edits, and custom video editing.

How do I request a custom edit?

Use the booking form to share your project details, service, timeline, and budget. The form prepares an email draft addressed to dipesh23.in@gmail.com.

How do I share my source footage?

Add a YouTube or Google Drive link in the Source Footage Link field. You can also include a reference video link for style direction.

How is the editing price determined?

Pricing is provided through a custom quote based on the project scope, duration, style, and delivery timeline. No fixed prices are listed.

How long will my edit take?

Delivery timing is agreed per project. Choose your desired delivery date in the booking form and confirm the final schedule by email.

Can I request changes or revisions?

Revision arrangements are discussed and agreed for each project. Include any important requirements in your project description.

How will I receive my completed video?

Delivery details are confirmed during the project discussion. Final files are shared through the agreed method.

How do I contact Dips Editz?

Use the booking form, email dipesh23.in@gmail.com, call +91 7305855341, or message on WhatsApp.
